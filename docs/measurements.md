# Measurement protocol

Do not change the cats' access to their boxes or provoke an elimination event for a
measurement. Start with the usual routine. This project does not diagnose health.
Keep identifiable household recordings in ignored `measurements/` or `recordings/`.

## Record equipment and uncertainty

For each instrument record its resolution and a simple repeatability check. Reweigh
the same object several times, rather than treating the printed scale resolution as
measurement accuracy. Record dates, litter product/lot, ambient temperature/humidity,
box ID, operator, units, and any missing observation. Missing values are not zero.

Measure inner floor and entrance dimensions, wall/plate thickness, slot width, length
and pitch, drawer depth/clearance, and any enclosure or pad. Photograph a ruler in the
same plane to avoid perspective errors. Measure both boxes; do not assume identical
placement or contents. Advertised product dimensions remain unverified priors.

Measure at least 30 randomly selected dry pellets for length and diameter. Use batch
mass divided by count if individual pellets are below the scale's resolution. Record
batch distribution and density calculation separately; bulk density is not solid wood
density. Record fill mass and depth at several positions, not just at a corner pile.

## Clean-material experiments

Repeat each condition at least three times, and retain individual results.

1. **Dry pour and repose:** use the same funnel/outlet height and mass. Capture the pile
   profile, final height/footprint and angle. Record preparation and boundary friction.
2. **Wetting:** dispense a weighed water dose into a weighed pellet bed. Record spatial
   wet footprint, retained liquid, drainage, and time. Use a fixed drainage protocol;
   a wet pellet's weight includes surface water, so label it retained water rather
   than pure absorption unless the measurement separates the two.
3. **Swelling and breakup:** take timed images with scale, then apply repeatable tool
   strokes with recorded depth/path/count. Measure separated intact and fine fractions
   with the same sieve protocol. Account for solids left on tools or walls.
4. **Sifting:** test dry and wet mixtures on an actual slot section. Record material
   above/below the pan before and after a defined disturbance. Include no-disturbance
   controls; gravity alone and stirring are different conditions.
5. **Drying:** measure exposed and covered controls over recorded elapsed time and
   humidity. Separate water loss from lost solids or drainage.
6. **Surrogate paste:** use a documented clean, reproducible gel/paste recipe and batch
   preparation. Record slump/spread, several imposed rates, and adhesion tests on clean
   steel and representative pellets. Slump alone cannot determine yield stress,
   consistency AND shear exponent. Preserve non-identifiable parameter intervals.

Water does not establish urine chemistry. Clean paste does not establish stool
properties. Keep those domain differences in every resulting calibration report.
No handling of actual waste for rheology is requested.

## Visits and maintenance

Use existing video or a fixed phone/camera, without new integrations or camera-account
credentials. Annotate cat/box ID, entry/exit, pre-dig/cover strokes, observed times,
location/direction, visible elimination class, no-cover/aborted events and uncertainty.
Do not infer invisible volume or paw force from a top-down recording.

Record scoop, stir, drawer-empty, refill and full-clean times and masses. Separate
added clean litter, drawer material, scooped waste and visible floor material. Wet
mixed-waste mass does not identify its individual species; compare total observable
mass unless independent separation is available. Use the normal safe waste-handling
routine, not additional exposure for the simulation.

Prefer 20-40 visits per cat. Split entire contiguous maintenance periods into fitting
and hold-out data BEFORE fitting; randomly splitting nearby visits leaks shared box
state. Report the number of independent cycles, not merely the number of frames.

## Acceptance records

Save raw observations and instrument uncertainty, fitted parameters and provenance,
withheld cycles, predictions, error definitions and failure cases. Near-zero errors
use absolute scale uncertainty instead of unstable relative percentages. Numerical
convergence and synthetic fixtures do not replace these physical measurements.

All current demonstration scenarios use assumed parameters. No measured household
calibration can be accepted until the above data are supplied.
