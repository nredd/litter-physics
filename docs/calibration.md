# Observations, calibration, and behaviour profiles

Data flow: measurements -> `ObservationSet` -> `CalibrationBundle` -> request
`materials` (plus provenance hash) -> run. Nothing in this chain can turn synthetic or
assumed numbers into a household accuracy claim. Every parameter carries units,
provenance, uncertainty, and a status: `measured`, `literature_prior`, or `assumed`.

## Observation file

`schemas/observations.schema.json`; example `examples/observations_synthetic.json`
(generated by `litter-physics observations synthesize`, flagged `synthetic: true`).

Material groups (each optional, each with `status`, `source`, `instrument_uncertainty`):

- `dry_pour`: mass, settled volume, repose angle per pour
- `uptake`: time series of absorbed water per dry mass, optional swelling ratio
- `breakup`: broken mass fraction after standardized agitation vs wet exposure time
- `slump`: initial/final height and spread of the clean surrogate paste
- `flow_curve`: steady shear stress at >= 4 shear rates
- `adhesion`: peak traction and separation per substrate

Logs:

- `visits`: per visit `cat_id`, `box_id`, start, inspect/dig/eliminate/cover
  durations, stroke counts, elimination kind, `abandoned`, optional deposit mass
- `maintenance`: time, kind, box, weighed removed mass, clean-litter loss, added mass

## What `calibrate` identifies

- `water_capacity_ratio`, `uptake_rate_s`: `curve_fit` of `W(1 - exp(-k t))` to the
  uptake series; uncertainty from the covariance, floored at instrument uncertainty
- `breakdown_rate_s`: `curve_fit` of `1 - exp(-k t)` to breakup trials; valid only at
  the trials' moisture and agitation
- `friction`: `tan(mean repose angle)` labelled `assumed` with a note. Repose constrains
  friction plus rolling resistance jointly; it is a prior, not an identification.
  Confirm against DEM repose tests in the mechanics module
- Herschel-Bulkley `yield_stress_pa`, `consistency_pa_s_n`, `flow_index`: fitted ONLY
  when a multi-rate `flow_curve` exists. Slump alone does not identify the three
  parameters; slump-only input leaves rheology empty and the report says so. Slump is
  reported as an independent check when both exist
- `restitution`, `normal_stiffness_n_m`, `evaporation_rate_s`: no protocol in the
  schema; always `assumed` from the template
- Adhesion is imported and validated but not mapped to a household parameter yet

Synthetic observations: every parameter is downgraded to `assumed` regardless of fit
quality and the bundle's `validation_claim` is `none`. The synthetic curves have known
closed-form truth (capacity 2.8, rate 0.12, breakdown 0.004, HB 30/10/0.6), which
`tests/test_calibration.py` uses to verify the fitting code, not the physics.

## Personalization scoring

Per cat, the last 30% of visits (contiguous in time) are held out. A lognormal on total
in-box duration is fitted per cat and on the pooled training visits of all cats; the
mean held-out log-likelihood decides. `supported` is true only when the per-cat model
beats the pooled baseline AND the cat has >= 20 training visits (`strong`). 5-19 visits
is `weak`; fewer is `insufficient`. Sensitivity of this score is not a confidence
interval and duration is the only feature scored today.

## Household mass comparison

`calibrate --run DIR` compares the run's final `removed` ledger mass (wood + waste +
water) with the sum of weighed `scoop`/`empty_drawer`/`clean` removals. Tolerance is
the larger of 20%, the removal repeatability (population std), and 1e-4 kg near zero.
This is a single aggregate check, not per-visit validation, and is meaningless with
synthetic observations (the report says so).

## Behaviour profiles and visit generation

`CatProfile` (`schemas/cat-profile.schema.json`) holds lognormal sojourns and counts,
stage probabilities (`p_abandon`, `p_skip_dig`, `p_repeat_dig`, `p_no_cover`,
`p_urine`, `p_soft_stool`), deposit mass, paw radius, source height, box weights, and
a preferred location. `profile_from_observations` fits these from a cat's visits;
`synthetic_profile` is a labelled placeholder.

Generation (`litter-physics visits`): one `numpy` child stream per cat from a master
`SeedSequence`, so adding a cat does not perturb another cat's draws. Stage chain
approach -> enter -> inspect -> dig (skippable, repeatable) -> posture -> eliminate ->
cover (skippable) -> exit, or inspect -> abandon -> exit. Each dig/cover stroke becomes
a wire `dig`/`cover` event with alternating direction and the paw radius; elimination
becomes `urinate` or `defecate` with a water fraction. Box choice applies occupancy
exclusion only: an occupied box makes the cat pick a free one or wait.

Not implemented (on purpose or pending evidence): cleanliness-driven box choice,
paw force inference from motion, RL or emotional state, developmental drift, visit
replay from video annotations (the observation schema stores stage durations, but a
per-stroke replay format does not exist yet).

## Collecting measurements

- Geometry: measure interior dimensions, slot width/length/pitch, plate thickness,
  drawer clearance with calipers; advertised dimensions are provisional
- Pellets: weigh and measure >= 50 pellets for size/mass distributions
- Dry pour: pour a weighed mass into a graduated container three times; measure the
  repose cone angle
- Uptake: immerse weighed pellets, weigh at fixed intervals until the mass plateaus
- Breakup: soak to a known moisture, agitate on a fixed protocol, sieve and weigh fines
- Surrogate paste: rheometer flow curve at >= 4 rates; slump with a standard cylinder;
  peel or probe adhesion on steel and wood coupons
- Household: camera-annotated visits with stage times and stroke counts (20-40 per
  cat preferred); weigh every scoop, drawer empty, top-up, and full clean

Keep raw measurements under ignored `measurements/`; commit only derived,
non-sensitive observation files.

## Failure examples

- `calibrate` with a slump-only file: bundle has no rheology and the diagnostics say
  "NOT identifiable from slump alone"
- `calibrate` with `synthetic: true`: report header `synthetic=True`, claim `none`
- `run --calibration synthetic-bundle.json`: warning "calibration bundle is
  SYNTHETIC; results are uncalibrated"; provenance still records the bundle hash
- `visits` with a research template: error, generation needs a household template

Synthetic personalization scores now carry `evidence=synthetic` and `supported=False`,
even if a per-cat fit beats the pooled baseline numerically. The log-likelihood values
remain useful pipeline diagnostics, but cannot establish evidence about actual cats.
Material calibration does not automatically validate either numerical model. The
current native household model does not consume research response tables.
